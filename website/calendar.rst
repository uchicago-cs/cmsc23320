Autumn 2026 Course Calendar
---------------------------

.. warning::

   This calendar is tentative and subject to change.

Lectures take place in Ryerson 251 on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 11:00am to 12:20pm.
Most weeks, the Tuesday lecture is used to introduce a new topic, and the Thursday lecture
is used to cover various aspects of the course projects. Lecture notes supplementing
the material covered that week are posted on Fridays.

Discussion sessions take place in Stuart 102 on Wednesdays from 4:30pm to 5:50pm.
Most weeks, the discussion session is used to administer one or more checkpoint exams,
followed by a discussion of the exam problems.

All deadlines are at 8pm (Chicago local time) on the specified date.
